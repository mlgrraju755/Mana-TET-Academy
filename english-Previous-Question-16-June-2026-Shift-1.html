Tourism is one of the fastest growing sectors of the global economy. It brings high income, foreign exchange, and supports infrastructure development. It also introduces new management and educational practices. These benefits help overall social and economic growth. Many developed countries like Switzerland, Austria, and France earn a large part of their income from tourism. Tourism contributes about 10% of the world's income and provides jobs to nearly one tenth of the global workforce. It creates employment in both big cities and small communities. In some places, it is the main economic activity. Tourism is often called an "industry without chimney" because it generates income without heavy pollution. It also helps balance foreign trade. In India, the idea of "Athithi Devo Bhava" shows the importance of guests. People treat guests with great respect and care, making proper arrangements for their comfortable stay.
